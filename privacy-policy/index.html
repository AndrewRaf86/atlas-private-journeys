Privacy policy

What this website collects

This website does not have a contact form, account system or database. You cannot submit personal information directly through the site. Contact happens through WhatsApp, which you initiate yourself.

Analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand overall traffic, such as how many people visit a page. Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not track you individually across sites.

Contact-link measurement

We count clicks on the official WhatsApp links using Vercel Web Analytics, along with the page, button position and a general source category such as Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or search. These custom event details do not include your WhatsApp message, name, phone number or other traveler information. A click does not tell us whether you sent a message.

To retain a general source category as you move between pages, we use session storage in your browser tab. We do not create a personal identifier or store raw referral links or arbitrary campaign text. This source category is not retained by this code after the tab session ends. This contact-click measurement is disabled when your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Language preference

If you choose Spanish or Portuguese with the language selector, your browser stores that choice in local storage on your device so the site can open in the same language on your next visit. The choice is not sent to Atlas or to analytics, contains no personal identifier, and can be cleared in your browser settings.

WhatsApp

When you message Atlas on WhatsApp, the conversation is handled by WhatsApp, owned by Meta, under its own privacy policy and terms. Information you share with Fidel—such as travel dates, group size or accommodation—is used to plan and coordinate your trip.

Data requests

If you would like to know what information Atlas has about you, or would like it deleted, message Fidel directly on WhatsApp.

Changes to this policy

If the way this website collects or uses information changes, this page will be updated.

Contact
