What this website collects

This website does not have a contact form, account system, or database. There is no way to submit personal information directly through the site. All contact happens through WhatsApp, which you initiate yourself when you message Fidel.

Analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand overall traffic — for example, how many people visit a page. Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not track you individually across sites.

WhatsApp

When you message Atlas on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by WhatsApp (owned by Meta) under WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms, not this website's. Information you share with Fidel over WhatsApp — such as your travel dates, group size, or accommodation — is used only to plan and coordinate your trip.
