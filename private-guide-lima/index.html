Where Fidel takes you

Lima has layers most tourists never reach. Fidel knows the city well and tailors the day to your group — whether that's history, food, architecture, or all three.

Huaca Pucllana

Ancient adobe pyramid in the middle of Miraflores. One of the most striking archaeological sites in Lima — especially at sunset.

Miraflores

Lima's upscale coastal district — clifftop parks, ocean views, and some of the city's best restaurants and cafés.

Barranco

Lima's bohemian arts neighborhood. Colorful streets, galleries, street murals, and a local restaurant scene that punches well above its weight.

Larco Mar

Clifftop shopping and dining complex overlooking the Pacific. A good stopping point for views and a meal with the ocean in front of you.

Historic Centre

Colonial architecture, grand plazas, and centuries of Peruvian history — the UNESCO-listed heart of Lima.
