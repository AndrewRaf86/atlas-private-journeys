05 / Questions

Build the
right day.

What can a private day include?

Miraflores, Barranco, historic Lima, markets, museums, coastal views, food, shopping, nightlife or a custom combination.

Can Atlas arrange transportation?

Atlas can coordinate private transportation between Lima Airport, Miraflores, Barranco, San Isidro and other agreed parts of the city.

Can Fidel help with restaurants and nightlife?

Yes. Restaurant, neighborhood, lounge, live music and evening transport planning can be discussed based on your group.

How do I get a quote?

Send your date, group size, available time and interests through WhatsApp. Fidel will discuss the itinerary and price directly.

Choose the Lima you want to see.

These are starting points for a conversation, not fixed itineraries or included packages. Fidel confirms the route, transportation, reservations, timing and price for your dates.

Coast and neighborhoods

Discuss Miraflores coastal views and time in Barranco. Tell Fidel whether you prefer walking, private transportation or a mix, and how much time you have available.

History and food

Ask about combining historic Lima with markets or a restaurant. Share your food preferences and any dietary requirements before discussing reservations. Meals, tickets and reservations are included only if confirmed in your arrangement.

An evening in the city

Discuss restaurants, lounges, live music or nightlife, along with your accommodation area and transport needs. Venue availability and admission are not guaranteed.

Send your date, group size, starting area and interests. If your day starts at the airport, arrange arrival support separately. For a celebration with friends, see Lima group coordination.
