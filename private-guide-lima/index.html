What can a private day include?

Miraflores, Barranco, historic Lima, markets, museums, coastal views, food, shopping, nightlife or a custom combination.

Can Atlas arrange transportation?

Atlas can coordinate private transportation between Lima Airport, Miraflores, Barranco, San Isidro and other agreed parts of the city.

Can Fidel help with restaurants and nightlife?

Yes. Restaurant, neighborhood, lounge, live music and evening transport planning can be discussed based on your group.

How do I get a quote?

Send your date, group size, available time and interests through WhatsApp. Fidel will discuss the itinerary and price directly.
