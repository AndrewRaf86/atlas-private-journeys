Questions

Common questions about guide days

How long is a guide day?+

Typically 8–10 hours, starting and ending at your accommodation. Fidel agrees on the timing with you in advance — it can be adjusted based on what you want to cover.

What's not included in the price?+

Meals, entrance fees, and personal purchases are paid separately. Fidel will tell you what to expect before the day so there are no surprises.

Can I tell Fidel what I want to see in advance?+

Yes — that's the point. Message Fidel on WhatsApp before the day and tell him your priorities. He'll build the route around them and suggest what fits well.

How many people can join a guide day?+

Up to around 6–8 people comfortably in a private vehicle. Larger groups are possible — mention your group size when you message Fidel and he'll accommodate accordingly.

Can I add a guide day to my airport transfer or bachelor party?+

Yes. Many people book a guide day as part of a longer trip with Fidel — combined with airport pickup, nightlife planning, or full group coordination. Tell Fidel what you're planning and he'll put together a quote for the whole thing.

How does payment work?+

Scope and price agreed over WhatsApp before any payment. You know exactly what's included before you commit.

Ready to see Lima properly?

Message Fidel with your dates, group size, and what you'd like to see. He'll confirm availability and put together a plan.
