Neighbourhoods and places Fidel knows well

The itinerary is built around your preferences — these are some of the areas Fidel covers.

Miraflores

Lima's clifftop district overlooking the Pacific — parks, markets, viewpoints, and the best ceviche.

Barranco

Lima's bohemian neighbourhood — colonial architecture, street art, galleries, and local bars.

Historic Centre

UNESCO-listed colonial city centre with plazas, churches, and centuries of Peruvian history.

Huaca Pucllana

An ancient adobe pyramid rising from the middle of the city — pre-Inca and often overlooked.

Frequently asked questions

Private guide day questions answered

How long is a private guide day?

You can book a half day (typically 4–5 hours) or a full day (typically 7–9 hours). Fidel will shape the itinerary to suit the time you have.

What neighbourhoods can we visit?

Fidel covers Miraflores, Barranco, the Historic Centre, Huaca Pucllana, and other areas across Lima. The itinerary is built around your interests — tell Fidel what you're most curious about.

Is food included?

Fidel will take you to great places to eat and drink and introduce you to dishes and markets worth knowing. Food and drink costs are paid separately at the venues you visit.

Is transport between neighbourhoods included?

Yes. Transport between the places you visit during the day is arranged and included as part of the service.

Can we customise the itinerary?

Entirely. Tell Fidel what you're interested in — food, art, history, architecture, markets — and he'll build the day around that. There's no fixed route.

How far in advance do we need to book?

As early as possible is best, especially during busy seasons. Message Fidel with your dates and he'll confirm availability. He also tries to accommodate last-minute requests when possible.
