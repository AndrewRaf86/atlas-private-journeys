Questions

Airport transfer FAQ

Which airport is this for?+

Jorge Chávez International Airport (LIM) in Callao, Lima — the main international airport for Peru.

How far is the airport from Miraflores?+

About 45–60 minutes by car depending on traffic. The drive can be longer during peak hours. Your driver knows the best routes.

What if my flight is delayed or arrives at night?+

The driver tracks your flight number. Delays are expected and managed — they wait. Night arrivals are no problem; this service is specifically useful for those situations.

How do I find my driver at the airport?+

Your driver will be in the arrivals hall holding a sign with your name. Before you fly, Fidel sends you the driver's name and phone number so you can confirm.

What group sizes do you handle?+

Solo travelers up to groups of 10+. For larger groups, multiple vehicles are arranged. Confirm your group size when you message Fidel and he'll quote accordingly.
