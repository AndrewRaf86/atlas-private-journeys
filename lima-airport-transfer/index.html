Frequently asked questions

Airport transfer questions answered

Which airport does this service cover?

Atlas transfers operate from Jorge Chávez International Airport (LIM) in Lima, Peru. Drop-off transfers from your Lima accommodation back to the airport are also available.

What if my flight is delayed?

Fidel monitors your flight in real time. If your arrival is delayed, he adjusts accordingly — no need to contact him about normal delays. Just let him know if your plans change significantly.

Can you accommodate large groups or lots of luggage?

Yes. When you message Fidel, share your group size and any luggage specifics. He'll arrange a vehicle suited to your party.

Where exactly will Fidel or the driver meet me?

At the arrivals exit at Jorge Chávez Airport, holding a sign with your name. Fidel will also confirm the exact meeting point with you via WhatsApp before your flight.

Can I book a transfer from my hotel to the airport for departure?

Yes — drop-off transfers are available. Share your departure date, flight time, and hotel address when you message Fidel.

How do I contact Fidel if something changes?

All communication is through WhatsApp. You'll have his number once you book, and he's responsive throughout your trip.
