01 / What is included

From arrivals to your door.

Landing in a new city should not start with guessing where to go, negotiating at the curb or wondering whether your driver is coming. Share your flight and accommodation details directly with Fidel before you travel.

The arrangement is especially useful for first-time visitors, families and groups arriving on one or several flights, and travelers who want a reliable first step in Lima.

Flight monitoring

Fidel follows normal flight changes and adjusts the pickup timing around your arrival.

Meet-and-greet

Clear pickup coordination so you know where to meet after you land.

Private transport

Direct travel from the airport to your agreed Lima accommodation.

One contact

Coordinate the details with Fidel through WhatsApp before departure and on arrival.

What information should I send?

Your flight number and airline, arrival date and time, number of travelers and accommodation address.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Fidel monitors normal flight updates and adjusts the pickup timing. Keep him updated through WhatsApp if your plans change.

Can Atlas coordinate several arrivals?

Yes. Share each flight and traveler group so Fidel can discuss a practical pickup plan and quote.

Is the price fixed on this website?

No. Pricing depends on the details of your transfer and is confirmed directly before you commit.
