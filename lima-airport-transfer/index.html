Questions

Common questions about airport transfers

Which airport do you serve?+

Jorge Chávez International Airport (LIM) in Callao — Lima's main international airport. All incoming international and domestic flights arrive here.

What if my flight is delayed?+

Fidel monitors your flight and keeps the driver updated. If your flight is delayed, the driver adjusts. Message Fidel on WhatsApp if anything changes — he's reachable before and during the pickup.

Can you pick up multiple people on different flights?+

Yes. Let Fidel know all the flight details and he'll coordinate timing and vehicles so your group ends up at the same accommodation without anyone waiting at the airport for hours.

Is it safe to arrive in Lima at night?+

Many flights arrive in Lima late at night. The key is knowing which transport to use and which route to take to your neighborhood. Having a private driver arranged in advance removes the risk of negotiating with unfamiliar taxis at midnight.

How does payment work?+

Scope and price are agreed over WhatsApp before any payment. You'll know exactly what's included and what you're paying before you confirm.

Can I add other services to the transfer?+

Yes. Many people book a transfer as part of a broader trip — adding a guide day, group coordination, or full itinerary support. Tell Fidel what you're planning and he'll put together whatever makes sense.

Send Fidel your flight details on WhatsApp. He'll confirm availability and send a quote.
