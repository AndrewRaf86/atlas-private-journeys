## Research standard applied

Every external claim used to make a recommendation in this growth mission is either (a) sourced to an official platform help page, (b) a direct, dated observation of a competitor's live site, or (c) explicitly marked as a search-result-level observation with lower confidence. No claim about Atlas itself (pricing, certifications, years in business, response times) was invented — all such items are routed to `15_OWNER_DECISIONS_REQUIRED.md` instead.
