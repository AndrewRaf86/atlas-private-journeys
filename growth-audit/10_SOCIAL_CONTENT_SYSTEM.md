# Social Content System

## Reality check before platform selection

Atlas currently has **no verified social account on any platform** (confirmed in `04_SEARCH_VISIBILITY_BASELINE.md`). Every recommendation below starts from zero. For a single-operator business, the constraint isn't which platforms exist — it's that Fidel's time is the scarcest resource, and every hour spent on content is an hour not spent on paying trips or responding to inquiries. The plan below is deliberately narrow.

## Platform fit assessment

| Platform | Recommend now? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | **Yes — primary** | Highest-intent platform for this exact audience (travelers researching Peru visually); also functions as a lightweight portfolio/trust page even at low posting frequency |
| WhatsApp Status/Channel | **Yes — free, near-zero effort** | Atlas already lives on WhatsApp with every client; a Channel or Status update reaches people already in a trust relationship with zero new audience-building required |
| YouTube / YouTube Shorts | Later | High value (long-form destination content ranks and ages well) but real production effort; revisit once Instagram content exists to repurpose |
| TikTok | Later, optional | Same content can often repurpose from Instagram Reels; not worth a separate content strategy for a single-operator business yet |
| Pinterest | Later, low effort once other content exists | Good for repurposing existing photos/itinerary content into search-discoverable pins; low ongoing effort |
| Facebook | Low priority | Audience skews away from Atlas's likely segment (younger/first-time travelers, bachelor groups); maintain a page for NAP consistency (name/address/phone matching GBP) but don't invest content effort |
| LinkedIn, Threads, Reddit (as a brand account) | Not recommended as brand accounts | Reddit specifically should be *participation*, not a branded posting account (see `09_FREE_AND_LOW_COST_GROWTH_PLAN.md`) |

**Primary content engine: Instagram, fed by real trip footage Fidel already has access to (he's present on every trip).** Repurpose into WhatsApp Status and, once a backlog exists, Pinterest.

## Content pillars (grounded in what Atlas can honestly show)

1. **Real itinerary breakdowns** — an actual day Fidel ran, with real neighborhoods/restaurants, not generic "top 10 Lima" listicle content.
2. **What a private day solves** — concrete before/after framing (e.g., "the group tour version of this stop vs. what we actually did").
3. **Honest logistics content** — airport pickup process, what bachelor-party groups actually need to know before arriving. This doubles as pre-purchase FAQ content and matches the existing site's honest, specific tone.
4. **Local access and context** — Fidel's actual local knowledge (a market, a neighborhood detail) that a generic listing can't replicate.
5. **Client moments (with permission only)** — reuse the same consent standard already required for testimonials; do not post any client photo/video without explicit permission tied to that specific piece of content.

Deliberately excluded as pillars right now: generic "why visit Peru" inspiration content (crowded, low differentiation, not tied to booking intent) and manufactured "day in the life" content that isn't from a real trip.

## Realistic starting cadence

**2-3 Instagram posts/week, repurposed to WhatsApp Status.** Not daily, not multi-platform simultaneously. A consistent, real, low-volume presence beats a burst that isn't sustainable against Fidel's actual capacity — this is the same principle as the single-operator capacity risk flagged in `01_BUSINESS_AND_OFFER_AUDIT.md`.

## First 4 weeks — concrete starting content (not a padded 12-week grid)

| Week | Post | Format | Source material | CTA / destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fidel introduces himself and what Atlas actually does | Short video, vertical | Filmed once, becomes the pinned/profile intro | Link in bio → homepage |
| 1 | Real airport pickup walkthrough | Photo carousel or short video | Next real airport pickup (with client permission for any visible people) | → `/lima-airport-transfer` |
| 2 | One real neighborhood breakdown (Barranco or Miraflores) | Carousel | Existing real photos + new commentary | → `/private-guide-lima` |
| 2 | "What a private guide day actually includes" | Short video | Reuse copy from the private-guide-lima service cards, translated to spoken/visual format | → `/private-guide-lima` |
| 3 | Real client moment (pending consent) — Ram B.'s group of 10 | Carousel, if consented | Existing testimonial + any available real photos | → `/lima-bachelor-party` |
| 3 | Honest FAQ: "How far in advance should you book a bachelor trip?" | Short video/text graphic | Reuse existing FAQ copy | → `/lima-bachelor-party` |
| 4 | Local food/restaurant spot Fidel actually recommends | Photo + caption | Requires new real footage | → `/private-guide-lima` |
| 4 | Recap/proof: "3 groups, 3 different Lima trips" | Carousel | Synthesizes weeks 1-3 | → homepage |

Beyond week 4: repeat the same pillar rotation with new real material as trips happen, rather than trying to plan 12 weeks of content for a business that doesn't yet have the trip volume to source it honestly.

## Measurement

Track via each platform's native insights (follower growth is a vanity metric and should not be the success measure — per the mission brief's own instruction). The metric that matters: **does a social post produce a WhatsApp click or a direct mention in an inquiry** ("saw you on Instagram"). This requires Fidel to note the source when logging inquiries (see `12_ANALYTICS_AND_LEAD_MEASUREMENT.md`), not a platform-side integration.
