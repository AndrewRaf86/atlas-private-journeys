## How to use this

- Fill it out the same day each week (recommend Monday, reviewing the prior week).
- The last four rows (problem / experiment / result / action) matter more than the numbers early on — they're what turns this from a report into a decision tool.
- Do not fill in a metric with a guess if the underlying tracking isn't live yet — leave it blank and note why in the notes column. A scorecard with honest blanks is more useful than one with invented numbers.
