## Positioning recommendation

**Strongest truthful category: "Private local fixer / travel concierge in Lima."**

Not "luxury Peru tour operator" (Atlas doesn't operate tours, has no packages, no fleet, no guide roster — it's one person coordinating). Not "destination management company" (no evidence of B2B/agency-facing operations). "Private guide" undersells the airport-transfer and bachelor-party-logistics parts of the business. "Local fixer" or "private travel concierge" honestly describes what the evidence shows: one trusted local contact who handles logistics, recommendations, and coordination via WhatsApp, scoped to Lima today.

This matches the current homepage language ("A real person, not a listing") reasonably well — the main correction needed is narrowing the implied geography (Peru & Brazil) to match actual capability (Lima), or building out real Rio content before making that claim at the same confidence level as the Lima pages.

## Segment implication (see `02_CUSTOMER_AND_MARKET_RESEARCH.md` for full detail)

Given a single-operator, Lima-only, no-package, no-storefront business with zero current online trust signals, the realistic near-term customer fit is **travelers who already have Peru travel booked and need a trusted Lima-specific logistics layer** (airport pickup, one guided day, or a group weekend) — not travelers doing top-of-funnel "is Peru right for me" research, and not travelers comparison-shopping full multi-week packaged itineraries against 15+ established competitors with real review histories.
