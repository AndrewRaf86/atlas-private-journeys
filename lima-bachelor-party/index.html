Frequently asked questions

Bachelor party planning questions answered

How many days do we need for a Lima bachelor party?

Most groups do 3–4 nights in Lima, which gives enough time for a full day of exploration, at least one big dinner, and a couple of nights out. Fidel can work with whatever time you have — shorter stays can be packed efficiently too.

What kinds of activities can you arrange?

Fidel can arrange group dining, nightlife, day tours, coastal activities, and city experiences. The specifics depend on your group's interests — tell him what you're into and he'll build a programme around that. He'll be upfront about what's genuinely available in Lima and what requires advance planning.

Can you handle airport pickups for the whole group?

Yes. If group members are arriving on different flights, Fidel can coordinate individual airport transfers so everyone arrives at the accommodation smoothly. Share the flight details when you message.

Do you work with all group sizes?

Message Fidel with your group size and he'll confirm what he can accommodate. He's experienced with small groups (4–6) and larger ones.

How far in advance should we get in touch?

As early as possible — particularly for popular dates and if your group has specific restaurant or venue requests. Ideally 4–8 weeks out, but Fidel will do his best with shorter notice too.

Is Lima safe for a group trip?

Miraflores and Barranco — where most visitors spend their time — are established neighbourhoods with plenty of tourists and group activity. Fidel will brief you on what to know before you arrive and is available throughout the trip if anything comes up.
