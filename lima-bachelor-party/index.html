Common questions about bachelor parties in Lima

How far in advance should we book?+

At least 4–6 weeks ahead gives Fidel enough time to secure accommodation, make restaurant reservations, and build a proper plan. Shorter lead times are possible — message Fidel and he'll tell you what's realistic.

What group sizes does this work for?+

Groups of 6–20+ are common. Tell Fidel your group size upfront — it affects accommodation options, vehicle capacity, and restaurant reservations.

Is Lima a good choice for a bachelor party?+

Yes — it's one of the best cities in South America. World-ranked dining, a serious nightlife scene, and a city that genuinely welcomes groups. It's also less expensive than most European or US destinations for the same quality of experience.

Is Lima safe for a group?+

Lima is very manageable when you know which neighborhoods to be in and use vetted private transport at night. Fidel's role is partly safety — he knows the city, the routes, and the venues. The group stays together and gets home reliably.

What does the $1,200 starting price cover?+

The starting price covers Fidel's planning and coordination time — building the itinerary, making reservations, managing logistics, and being on-call throughout the weekend. Accommodation, restaurant bills, venue spend, and transport costs are separate and confirmed with you in advance. The total scope and breakdown are always agreed over WhatsApp before anything is paid.

Can we add a guide day or airport pickup?+

Yes — many groups arrive a day early and add a guide day to see Lima properly before the weekend kicks off. Airport pickups for the whole group are part of the standard coordination. Tell Fidel what you want and he'll put together a quote for the complete trip.
