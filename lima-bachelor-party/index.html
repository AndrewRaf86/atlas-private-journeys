01 / Why Lima

Food. Coast. Nightlife. Together.

Lima combines a serious food scene, Pacific coastline and active neighborhoods for evenings out. With a group, the difference is having transport, timing and reservations considered together.

What can Fidel coordinate?

Group dining, nightlife, private transport, city experiences, coastal options and airport pickups on request. The exact plan depends on your group and dates.

Can people arrive on different flights?

Yes. Share each arrival so Fidel can discuss an efficient transfer plan for the group.

What should we send first?

Your dates, group size, arrival details, food priorities, nightlife interest, activity level and any dietary needs.

How early should we plan?

As early as possible for popular dates or specific venue requests. Fidel will confirm what is realistic for shorter notice.
