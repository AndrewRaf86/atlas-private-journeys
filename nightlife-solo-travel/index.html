Atlas coordinates private tours, transportation, restaurants, nightlife and group travel in Lima and Brazil. Fidel is your direct contact. This guide helps you decide what to ask for; it is not a fixed itinerary, price list or guarantee of venue entry.

Start with the city. Then choose your pace.

Lima & Miraflores, Peru

Combine private city time with restaurants and an evening out. Discuss Miraflores, Barranco, historic Lima, food or coastal views, depending on your interests. Tell Fidel where you are staying and whether you need transport between agreed stops.

For a first arrival, arrange the airport pickup as its own part of the plan. Then explore private Lima guiding. A celebration with friends belongs in the Lima bachelor-party and group plan.

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

Tell Fidel how you want to balance beach time, private tours, dining and nightlife. Share your arrival details, accommodation area and group size so the transport and evening arrangements can be discussed together.

Start with private Brazil travel, or use the Brazil bachelor-party page for a group celebration. The actual venues, reservations and quote depend on your dates.

São Paulo, Brazil

Discuss restaurants, city interests, daytime activities and nights out as a separate city plan. If you visit both Rio and São Paulo, list the dates and flights for each. Do not assume that travel between cities or the same arrangements in both cities are included.

A good night starts before you leave.

Whether you are planning one evening or a group weekend, agree these details directly rather than leave them as assumptions:

  • Your atmosphere: dinner, bars, lounges, live music, a social evening or a group celebration.
  • Your timing: which date, how much time you have and whether the plan connects to a daytime tour.
  • Your transport: the pickup area, agreed stops and return arrangements you want coordinated.
  • Your scope: what the quote covers, reservations, any separate charges and the applicable change terms.

Meals, drinks, tickets, admission and transport are included only when agreed. Venue availability and entry are not guaranteed. Confirm the details before committing; read the service terms and change and cancellation information.

Researching a “passport bros” trip?

You do not need a particular travel label to ask for help. Solo travelers and friends researching passport bros travel can discuss the same practical services: airport arrivals, private tours, restaurants, nightlife and transportation.

Atlas is a travel-coordination service, not a dating or matchmaking service. Dates, introductions and personal connections are not included as bookable services or guaranteed outcomes. Meeting people depends on mutual interest and consent.

A useful first message is about the trip itself: where you are going, when, how many people are traveling and what you want coordinated. That lets Fidel confirm practical options rather than promise an experience that has not been agreed.

Colombia, Mexico or somewhere else?

Lima and Brazil are Atlas’s main focus. Colombia and Mexico requests are welcome, but city coverage and local arrangements must be confirmed directly with Fidel. Send your proposed city, dates, group size and priorities before making plans around Atlas’s involvement.

Planning to party in South America as part of a longer trip? Keep the dates and transport for each city separate. Mexico can be discussed as part of a wider Latin America trip, with arrangements confirmed city by city.
