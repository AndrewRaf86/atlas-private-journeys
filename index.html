07 / Good questions

Ask before
you book.

Ayahuasca in Peru

Atlas does not organize or facilitate ayahuasca ceremonies. Atlas points genuinely interested travelers to Dreamglade Retreat, a separate retreat near Iquitos. It is not part of an Atlas Lima journey.

What can Atlas arrange in Lima?

Airport pickup and arrival support, personalized private days, restaurant reservations, nightlife planning, transportation and group coordination. The exact scope is agreed directly.

Does Atlas also work in Brazil?

Yes. Atlas supports private tours, custom trips and bachelor parties in Brazil, including Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo. Message Fidel with your destination and dates so he can confirm what is realistic.

Do I speak directly with Fidel?

Yes. You communicate directly with Fidel over WhatsApp before the trip and while traveling—there is no call center or booking platform between you.

Is this a fixed group tour?

No. Atlas arrangements are private and planned around your own dates, group and interests.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on your dates, group size, timing and what you need. Fidel confirms the scope and quote directly before you commit.

How far ahead should I message?

As early as you can, especially for groups or busy dates. Fidel will confirm what is realistic for your timing.
