Questions

Good questions to ask first

What exactly does Atlas do?+

Atlas is your local contact in Peru and Brazil. We handle airport pickups, private transport, guide services, restaurant and nightlife planning, group coordination, and custom itineraries — with WhatsApp support before and during your trip.

Is this just a tour company?+

No. You get one real local who coordinates the whole trip and stays reachable — not a fixed group tour or a list of packaged excursions. Everything is built around your dates, group, and pace.

Do you work with bachelor parties?+

Yes — frequently. The focus is logistics and safety: group transport, restaurant and nightlife reservations, a clear night-by-night plan, and one local contact so the whole group stays together and gets home safely.

Is Lima safe for tourists?+

Lima is very manageable when you know which areas to be in and how to get around. Having a local arrange your transport and routes — instead of relying on random taxis — removes most of the situations that catch first-time visitors off guard.

How does payment work?+

We agree on the scope and a clear quote over WhatsApp before any payment. You always know what's included before you commit.

Can you help with airport pickup?+

Yes. Private airport pickup is one of the most requested services — a known driver waiting for you on arrival and a direct transfer to your stay, no airport-taxi guesswork.

Can you help with nightlife?+

Yes. We plan restaurants and nightlife around your group — booking ahead at the right venues and arranging safe transport between them.

Do you plan Brazil trips too?+

Yes — Atlas covers Brazil alongside Peru, with a focus on Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo. Message Fidel with your dates and we'll plan from there.

Do you arrange ceremonies or retreats?+

Our core focus is logistics, transport, guides, dining, and nightlife. If you have a specific request such as a wellness or ceremony experience, ask Fidel directly and he'll let you know what he can responsibly arrange.
